# 📲 F&O Breakout Screener — App Launcher & 1-Click Setup Guide

Welcome to the **F&O Breakout & Fibonacci Screener System**. 
Follow the quick 1-click instructions below for your device to open or install the screener as an app on your home screen or desktop.

---

## 📱 Mobile Phones & Tablets

### 1. Android (Google Chrome / Brave / Edge)
1. Open the live link in Chrome: **[https://f-o-breakout-screener-system.streamlit.app](https://f-o-breakout-screener-system.streamlit.app)**
2. Tap the **three dots menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **"Install App"** (or **"Add to Home Screen"**).
4. The **F&O Screener** icon will now appear on your phone's home screen alongside your trading apps (Zerodha, Groww, etc.). Tapping it launches the screener in fullscreen without browser bars.

---

### 2. iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

#### Method A: 1-Click Profile Install (Automatic)
1. Open the `iPhone_iPad/FO_Screener.mobileconfig` file on your iPhone (or email/WhatsApp it to yourself).
2. iOS will show: *"Profile Downloaded"*.
3. Go to **Settings ➔ Profile Downloaded ➔ Install**.
4. The **F&O Screener** app icon will instantly appear on your iPhone Home Screen!

#### Method B: Standard Safari 2-Tap Setup
1. Open **[https://f-o-breakout-screener-system.streamlit.app](https://f-o-breakout-screener-system.streamlit.app)** in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

---

## 💻 Desktop (Windows & Mac)

### 1. Windows PC (1-Click Launchers)
Go into the `Windows/` folder:
- **Option A (Desktop Shortcut Installer):** Double-click `Install_Desktop_Shortcut.bat`. It will instantly create a high-resolution shortcut on your Windows desktop.
- **Option B (Direct Launcher):** Double-click `Launch_Screener.bat` or `FO_Breakout_Screener.url`.
- **Option C (Chrome / Edge PWA):** Open the link in Chrome or Edge, click the **Install** icon (⊕ or 💻) on the right side of the URL bar, and click **Install**.

---

### 2. Apple Mac (macOS)
Go into the `Mac/` folder:
- **Option A (Mac App):** Double-click `FO_Breakout_Screener.app` (you can also drag it into your **Applications** folder or **Dock**).
- **Option B (Terminal Command):** Double-click `Launch_Screener.command`.
- **Option C (Safari Web App):** Open the link in Safari on macOS Sonoma/Sequoia, go to **File ➔ Add to Dock**.
